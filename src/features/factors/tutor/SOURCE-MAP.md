## Teaching adaptations and source clarification

- The N7.1 video demonstrates 60, which is Q4a rather than Q1. It opens the section with a matching interactive reconstruction; the PDF’s Q1 factor tree for 84 follows as a separate worked screen.
- N7.1 Q5b is internally contradictory: `96 = 2⁵ × 3¹` has no even exponent, although the prompt says one exponent is even. The learner screen preserves the intended misconception by stating that the student has confused the even prime base 2 with an even exponent. The correction still teaches that every prime exponent must be even for a square number.
- Factor trees use the source’s smallest-prime-first chain, but the explanation notes that other valid factor pairs reach the same prime factorisation.
- Venn working matches repeated prime copies one at a time. The video’s shorthand “multiply the circled numbers” is made precise: multiply only the intersection for HCF and every region in the union for LCM.
- Q5a and Q5b for p and q show the lower/higher exponent rule through the same Venn placement.
- Marks and difficulty badges are omitted. MP4 files are bundled unchanged under `public/media/lesson-7/`; posters come from the supplied clips.
