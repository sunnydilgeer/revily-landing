# Lesson 1 Variant B — the number family

## Experiment

Variant B replaces Variant A’s opening number-classification sequence with a visual model of the number family, then rejoins Variant A at factors and uses the same remaining lesson states.

The family model shows the mathematically accurate relationships:

- rational numbers contain integers and rational non-integers;
- integers contain whole numbers;
- irrational numbers sit outside the rational numbers.

Each region contains examples that are separate from the current question’s choices: whole numbers `0, 1, 2, 3, 4…`; other integers `−2, −7`; rational non-integers `½, −1.5`; irrational numbers `π, √2`.

The reference is Math Monks’ use of separate whole-number and integer number lines plus a nested-set diagram. Revily uses the mathematical ideas but original wording, interactions and code-native visuals; no source images or prose are reproduced.

## Six-screen opening

1. **Hook:** identify an integer that is not a whole number. The family diagram remains visible as a teaching reference, while the selectable answers appear once in the standard answer area. Their diagram placements remain hidden until submission.
2. **Worked visual:** whole numbers begin at zero and extend right. Negative numbers, fractions and decimals are visibly outside the set.
3. **Worked visual:** extend the line left to form the integers. Fractions and non-integer decimals remain outside.
4. **Worked visual:** place examples across whole numbers, other integers, rational non-integers and irrational numbers.
5. **Guided check:** identify a negative integer among zero, a positive integer, a fraction and a negative decimal. Each single-choice distractor has tailored feedback.
6. **Independent check:** resolve expressions and select all whole-number values. Placements stay hidden until submission.

## Behaviour contract

- Incorrect answers reveal the accepted answer and never block progress.
- The action after feedback is `Continue`.
- Every interactive state follows the same reading order: visual, complete question, response controls.
- The guided integer prompt is one question: `Which value is an integer but not a whole number?`
- The diagram’s teaching examples remain visible, but the current question’s placements are absent from both the visual and accessible description before submission.
- The whole-number definition is zero plus positive integers; integers add negative integer values.
- Variant A shares the visual-first question layout and the upgraded factors/multiples and prime sections; its opening sequence remains intact.
- Lesson 2 remains unchanged.

## Shared visual upgrades

- Factors and multiples open with a side-by-side comparison anchored on 10: the factor side shows `1 × 10 = 10` and `2 × 5 = 10`, while the multiples side builds `10, 20, 30, 40…` in equal groups of 10.
- The prime introduction uses a 1–50 grid with every prime highlighted. Every number is hoverable and keyboard-focusable, revealing its complete factor list and whether it is prime, composite, or neither.
- Every grid cell is a keyboard-focusable control and exposes its factor evidence on hover or focus.
