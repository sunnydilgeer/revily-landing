# Lesson 14 source map

Lesson 14 is written from the six PDF/video pairs in `N14_Standard_Form`. The rungs put large numbers before small ones (the worksheets start with small), because positive powers are easier.

| Rung | Source PDF and video | App coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Into large numbers | `N14.2_Standard_Form_into_Large_Numbers` | Q1 video + worked example (3.6 × 10⁴, with the hop picture); Q2, Q3, Q4a, Q4b; Q5a (find n); Q5b (any number between, checked by range); Q5c (Ali) as a choice |
| Into small numbers | `N14.1_Standard_Form_into_Small_Numbers` | Q1 video + worked example (6.3 × 10⁻⁵); Q2–Q5b as above; Q5c (Priya) as a choice |
| Write large numbers | `N14.3_Writing_Large_Numbers_in_Standard_Form` | Q1 video + worked example (84 300 000); Q2–Q4b and Q5a (2.4 million) with the two-box answer; Q5b; Q5c (Maya) |
| Write small numbers | `N14.4_Writing_Small_Numbers_in_Standard_Form` | Q1 video + worked example (0.000512); Q2–Q5a (virus, 10⁻¹⁰) with the two-box answer; Q5b; Q5c (Jayden) |
| Multiply | `N14.5_Multiplying_Standard_Form` | Q1 video + worked example; Q2–Q5a with the two-box answer; Q5b (pick the pair) and Q5c (Zara) as choices |
| Divide | `N14.6_Dividing_Standard_Form` | Q1 video + worked example; Q2–Q5a with the two-box answer; Q5b (pick the division) and Q5c (Kwame) as choices |

Source answers are kept exactly. The final screen is an app-only summary. Practice questions (`maths/practice/bank/standardForm.ts`) use new numbers; their AQA weights in `aqaWeights.ts` are estimates until the 2022–25 papers are counted for standard form.
