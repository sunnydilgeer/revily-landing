# Lesson 16 (Algebra A2) source map

A2 is written from the eight PDF/video pairs in `A2_Powers_and_Roots`. It is lesson 16 in the course (`?lesson=16`, progress key `L016`), and students see it as lesson 2 of the Algebra chapter.

The rungs are taught in a different order from the PDFs, so each builds on the one before: power 1 (what a power means: copies), multiplying and dividing (counting and cancelling copies), power 0 (follows from dividing), 1 to any power, power of a power, fractions, then roots (a root undoes a power).

Five videos work different numbers from their PDF (A2.1, A2.2, A2.3, A2.5, A2.8), and A2.4's pattern uses 3s where the PDF uses 2s. As in A1, the video's example is the rung's worked screen and the PDF's worked example (Q1) becomes a practice question, so nothing is lost. A2.6's video includes the PDF's cube (1³) as its story; that is kept as practice too. A2.7's video works the PDF's own worked example, so that screen covers Q1.

Source answers are kept exactly. Small wording changes for the app: A2.2 Q3 and Q5a write the fraction line as ÷ (the working draws it as a fraction); A2.5 Q5b adds "in the form xⁿ"; A2.7 Q5b repeats 49/16 so it stands on its own; A2.8 Q4b's working said "check by squaring 24·5" then squared 7, so the app says 24·5 × 24·5 is about 600. Negative whole-number powers (7⁻³, 3a⁻⁴b, a⁻⁸) stay: integer powers are in the Foundation specification. The final screen is an app-only summary. Practice questions (`maths/practice/bank/indices.ts`) use new numbers; their AQA weights are estimates until the 2022–25 papers are counted for index laws.
