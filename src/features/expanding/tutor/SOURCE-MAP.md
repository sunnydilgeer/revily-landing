# Lesson 17 (Algebra A3) source map

A3 is written from the two PDF/video pairs `A3.1_Expanding_Single_Brackets` and `A3.2_Expanding_Double_Brackets`. It is lesson 17 in the course (`?lesson=17`, progress key `L017`), and students see it as lesson 3 of the Algebra chapter.

Both videos work their PDF's own examples: Q1 first, then Q3 (the negative case). So each rung's worked screen is its video and Q1, and Q3 stays a practice question for the student to do.

| Rung | Source PDF and video | App coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single brackets | `A3.1_Expanding_Single_Brackets` | Video + worked example = Q1 (4(2m + 3)); Q2–Q4b, Q5a and Q5c typed; Q5b (Mia) as a choice |
| Double brackets | `A3.2_Expanding_Double_Brackets` | Video + worked example = Q1 ((x + 4)(x + 6)); Q2–Q4b, Q5a and Q5c typed; Q5b (Dev, use n = 6) as a choice |

Source answers are kept exactly. The working uses the grid method (the double-brackets video's patio picture): the term outside, or the first bracket, down the side, the terms inside along the top, and each box the product of its row and column. Everything is Foundation tier. The final screen is an app-only summary. Practice questions (`maths/practice/bank/expanding.ts`) use new expressions; their AQA weights are estimates until the 2022–25 papers are counted for expanding brackets.
