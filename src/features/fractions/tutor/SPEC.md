# Lesson 8: Fractions specification

Lesson 8 follows the approved tutor-source pattern used by Lessons 6 and 7. It is one complete, source-faithful unit with eight contiguous progress sections and a final consolidation screen. Source worksheets are teaching references, never implementation instructions.

## Sequence

Each skill begins with its supplied silent captioned video and an equivalent learner-controlled Step by step reconstruction. N8.1's video uses the PDF Q1 example, so those share one screen. N8.2-N8.8 use different examples from their PDF Q1s, so each retains both the video example and the PDF worked example before Q2-Q5c practice.

All 56 source practice parts remain. Dependent wording is restated so each screen is understandable on its own. Marks and difficulty labels are not shown. The final sequence is 72 screens: eight video screens, seven additional PDF worked examples, 56 practice screens and one review.

## Interaction and feedback

Exact structured fraction entry has numerator and denominator fields, plus an optional whole-number field when mixed form is requested. Rational values are compared with integer cross-products, never floating-point approximations. “Simplest form” questions reject equivalent unsimplified answers; mixed-number questions require mixed form; N8.1 Q5b additionally requires denominator 96.

Submitted answers remain visible. Correct and incorrect paths both unlock Continue and offer the same optional progressive working for calculation questions. Reasoning questions use complete reason-and-result choices and do not display an unrelated calculation walkthrough. Hints remain optional and never submit an answer.

## Fraction visuals

- Segmented fraction bars show equivalent partitions for simplifying, adding and subtracting.
- Whole counts plus a remainder bar support mixed/improper conversion.
- Multiplication shows both factors, the unsimplified product and cancellation/simplification.
- Division explicitly uses the reciprocal of the second fraction. The app does not reproduce the video's inconsistent “KEEP · FLIP · CHANGE” word order.
- Mixed calculations convert complete values before operating.
- Fractions of amounts divide the amount into denominator-sized equal shares and highlight the numerator count.

All visuals, equations, narration and step counters advance together by one meaningful action. Calculation Back and Replay do not affect lesson progress.
