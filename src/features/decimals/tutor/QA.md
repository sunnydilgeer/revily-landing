# Lesson 6 QA

## Content

- All four source PDFs are covered in order: addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
- All 28 practice parts and all four Q1 worked examples are retained.

- Every source video has a Watch video / Step by step tab pair. Its walkthrough appears only in that tab and is not duplicated on the following screen.
- Decimal columns, carried values, regrouping, whole-number products and equivalent divisions reveal one completed action per click.
- Diagram, highlighted equation, narration and step counter use the same completed step.
- Lesson 6 answer feedback is compact: correctness plus an optional expandable progressive calculation, without the separate numbered explanation block.
- The four conceptual claim questions do not show a calculation walkthrough button.
- Back, section navigation, replay and keyboard focus use the shared Lessons 4-5 tutor shell.
