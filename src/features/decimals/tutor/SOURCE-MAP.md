## Teaching adaptations

- Each video appears before a learner-controlled reconstruction of the same Q1 calculation.
- Addition and subtraction use aligned decimal columns and explicit trailing zeroes. Subtraction uses “regroup” in tutor narration while retaining the source method.
- Multiplication is stated as “calculate the digits as a whole-number multiplication, then restore place value.” This avoids treating “ignore decimal points” as a general rule while preserving the source algorithm.
- Division multiplies the dividend and divisor by the same power of ten. The scaled calculation is kept visible before the quotient is built.
- Q4b and Q5b screens use the verified result from the preceding part rather than propagating a learner error.
- Reasoning questions use complete reason-and-example choices. Marks and difficulty badges are not displayed.
