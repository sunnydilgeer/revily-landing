The app preserves the source order, contexts, answers and the rule that a cut-off digit of 5 rounds up. Answers such as `3.50`, `7.0`, `0.10` and `6.00` use formatting-aware grading so their trailing zeroes continue to communicate the requested accuracy. The final screen is an app-only consolidation summary.
