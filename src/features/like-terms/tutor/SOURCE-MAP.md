# Lesson 15 (Algebra A1) source map

A1 is written from the four PDF/video pairs in `A1_Collecting_Like_Terms`. It is lesson 15 in the course (`?lesson=15`, progress key `L015`), and students see it as lesson 1 of the Algebra chapter.

Each video works a different example from its PDF. The video's example is the rung's worked screen, and the PDF's worked example (Q1) becomes a practice question, so nothing is lost.

| Rung | Source PDF and video | App coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One letter | `A1.1_Linear_Expressions_with_a_Single_Variable` | Video + worked example (3p + 5 + 2p + 4); "like terms with 3x?" (extra); Q1–Q5b typed; Q5c (Zara, 5w³) as a choice |
| Different letters | `A1.2_Like_Terms_with_Different_Letters` | Video + worked example (4ab + 3a + 2ab); "like terms with 4ab?" (extra); Q1–Q5b typed; Q5c (Omar) typed |
| Powers | `A1.3_Identifying_Like_Terms_with_Powers` | Video + worked example (3x²y + 2xy² + 4x²y); "like terms with 3x²y?" (extra); Q1–Q5b typed; Q5c (Leo, u²v²) as a choice |
| Letters and powers | `A1.4_Multiple_Letters_and_Powers` | Video + worked example (5mn + 3x + 2mn + 4y); "like terms with 5mn?" (extra); Q1–Q5b typed; Q5c (Freya) as a choice |

Source answers are kept exactly. Extra questions are marked `A1 extra practice`. The final screen is an app-only summary. Practice questions (`maths/practice/bank/likeTerms.ts`) use new expressions; their AQA weights are estimates until the 2022–25 papers are counted for simplifying.
