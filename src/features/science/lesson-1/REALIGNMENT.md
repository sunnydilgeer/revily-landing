Existing IDs remain stable where content is unchanged; increment content version because scope and final written task change. Old browser data remains stored under its old version, never regraded against new prompts.

Teach typical cells and exceptions: plant cells often have chloroplasts/vacuoles; do not classify from shape alone or missing chloroplasts. Bacteria have no nucleus but do have genetic material, membrane, cytoplasm, wall and ribosomes; plasmids may be present. Plant walls contain cellulose, bacterial walls do not. Areas are approximate, with square units and supplied actual dimensions (not measuring an unscaled schematic).
