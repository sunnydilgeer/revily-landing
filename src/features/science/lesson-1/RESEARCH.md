# Lesson 1 research and assessment alignment

Researched 13 September 2026. Target: **AQA GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy, Foundation Tier, 8464F**; Biology Paper 1 component 8464/B/1F. This is not Synergy (8465) or separate GCSE Biology (8461).

**Scope update, 14 September:** version 0.2.0 supersedes the original animal-only scope below. [Current alignment](./ALIGNMENT-2026-09-14.md) maps the supplied Cells page and whole-course chapter groups to official AQA content. Lesson 1 now includes plant/bacterial structures, classification, sizes/units/standard form, area estimates and written comparison. Earlier animal-only sequencing decisions are historical, not the current curriculum plan.

## Specification authority

[AQA assessment structure](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/specification-at-a-glance): six papers, two per science; each is 70 marks and 75 minutes. Biology Paper 1 includes Cell Biology. Splitting Revily's curriculum into three strands matches that assessment organisation; learners should still get an automatically selected next activity.

[AQA Biology content](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/biology-subject-content), **4.1.1.1–4.1.1.2**: nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria and ribosomes form this lesson's core. Learners must connect structures to functions, not just memorise diagram labels. Coverage here is partial: plants, bacteria, cell sizes and estimation are deferred, not marked complete. All core content selected here is accessible to Foundation; Foundation must not be equated with recall-only teaching.

[Working scientifically](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/working-scientifically), **WS 1.2, 3.5, 4.1**: interpreting models, drawing conclusions from evidence and using vocabulary motivate the model and observation screens. These links are design mappings, not official AQA AO classifications for Revily questions.

[Practical assessment](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/practical-assessment), **required practical 1 / AT 7**: microscope observations, labelled drawings and a magnification scale belong in the follow-on sequence. Lesson 1 only introduces the distinction between a schematic model and observations. It does not deliver or certify the required practical. Digital learning cannot replace the required hands-on activities.

Only the 2022 **mark scheme**, not its corresponding question paper, was successfully retrieved. Attempts to retrieve the 2024 Biology paper/mark scheme directly were unsuccessful; no 2024 claims are used. The 2023 question text and matching marking guidance were inspected; visual fetching of the question-paper pages failed, so this scaffold does not depend on an exact reproduction or interpretation of those figures. Source visual verification remains a teacher-review item if actual paper figures are later used.

Q01.2 involves specialised cells: this first lesson builds a prerequisite, not full coverage of that question. Q01.3 involves plant comparison: it belongs primarily in B-CELL-002. We should not generalise a figure-specific acceptance of missing chloroplasts into “all plant cells have chloroplasts”. Lesson 1 does not ask learners to classify an unknown cell as animal merely from its outline, nucleus or lack of chloroplasts.

## Limits and provenance

All prototype teaching, questions, options, feedback and visual briefs are **original Revily drafts**. They imitate relevant assessment demands, not AQA wording or artwork. Revily marks and AO tags are provisional design annotations; this is not an official AQA paper, endorsed resource, exhaustive past-paper analysis or forecast of what will appear next.

Use specification coverage as the curriculum authority and past papers as calibration. Do not exclude specification content because it was absent from the small sample inspected. Re-check sources when publishing; obtain appropriate rights before embedding actual exam questions or diagrams. No protected recent assessment materials were sought.
