The cell content used here is not marked Higher-tier-only in AQA. Sizes are explicit illustrative examples, not fixed diameters for every cell. Area uses supplied actual dimensions and an approximate rectangle, with µm² units. It does not measure an unscaled drawing or confuse diameter ratios with area ratios.

Version 0.3.0 reorders teaching around complete animal, plant and bacterial cells. Shared structures and category names are observations after the relevant cells are taught. AQA content coverage is unchanged; this is a Revily teaching-order decision, not an AQA-mandated sequence. The official cell-structure requirements were rechecked during the redesign.

Preserve qualifiers: most animal cells; plant cells often have chloroplasts/vacuoles; bacterial plasmids may occur. Plant/algal walls contain cellulose, bacterial walls do not. No shape-only classification or no-chloroplasts-means-animal inference. No nucleus does not imply no DNA/ribosomes. No claims that respiration creates energy or that colours are natural observations.

The existing inspected 2023 Biology 1F references remain prerequisite/application examples, not evidence that the newly authored questions will occur in a future paper. Every “Exam-style” label denotes a Revily draft. Written comparisons are saved pending teacher review, not keyword-graded. Counting no nucleus and DNA not enclosed in a nucleus twice is explicitly rejected by the rubric.

## Whole-course topic map

[AQA assessment groups](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/specification-at-a-glance) match the supplied contents: Biology B1–4 / B5–7; Chemistry C1–5 / C6–10; Physics P1–4 / P5–7. `curriculum.ts` maps all 24 topic codes to specification sections. AQA numbers the topics globally 1–24, whereas B/C/P chapter codes are the supplied book’s organisation. Do not confuse the two schemes.

Working scientifically, maths and practical assessment are cross-cutting, not an extra exam paper. The book’s subchapter divisions (B2a/b, B6a/b, C1a/b, P5a/b) fit within their parent topic groups; no missing new top-level topic is implied.

Only Cells Lesson 1 is built. Microscopy and magnification follow next; specialisation, differentiation, cell division and transport remain later B1 work. The observation scenario is illustrative, not a micrograph, apparatus method or required-practical completion. Detailed future lessons still need Foundation/HT-only filtering, prerequisite checks and teacher review. This mapping is not a claim that the entire qualification has been implemented.
