## Lesson 10 — The lungs

- Required air route: trachea, bronchi and alveoli. Smaller bronchioles remain labelled supporting anatomy but are not assessed as a required recall term.
- Gas exchange: oxygen diffuses from alveolar air into blood; carbon dioxide diffuses from blood into alveolar air.
- Adaptations: large total surface area, thin exchange surfaces, ventilation and blood flow maintaining concentration gradients.
- Breathing-rate arithmetic has been removed from the core lesson. The required blood-flow-rate calculation remains in Lesson 12.

## Lesson 11 — Circulatory system: the heart

- Learners trace one continuous route through the chambers and named vessels before that route is summarised as two linked circuits.
- Four chambers and the specified aorta, vena cava, pulmonary artery, pulmonary vein and coronary arteries.
- Heart-valve function without teaching valve names, which are outside the required scope.
- The left ventricle’s thicker wall is linked to pumping around the body.
- Natural pacemaker cells are located in the right atrium; artificial pacemakers are presented as electrical devices that correct some irregular rhythms, without treatment guarantees.

## Lesson 12 — Circulatory system: blood vessels

- Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood towards it. Oxygen content is not used as the definition, avoiding the pulmonary-vessel exception trap.
- Thick muscular/elastic artery walls, relatively large vein lumens and valves, and one-cell-thick capillary walls are linked to function.
- Capillary exchange is represented as an original schematic, not as a micrograph or direct observation.
- Blood-flow rate uses original values with `rate = volume ÷ time` and explicit units.
- Red cells, white cells, platelets and plasma appear on source page 25, not page 24, and are deliberately reserved for a later lesson.

## Anatomy redraw — 22 September 2026

All anatomy visuals in Lessons 10–12 now route to the original SVG components in `components/anatomy/`. The earlier placeholder drawings and anatomy text-only cards were removed. A later flow review reordered the heart route, removed off-spec breathing-rate arithmetic and raised the revised lesson content versions.

- Lungs: front-view lobes and cardiac notch, supported trachea, connected branching airways, enlarged alveolar sacs with a capillary mesh, and a separate section through the alveolar–capillary barrier.
- Heart: connected chamber inlets/outlets, open atrioventricular passages, separate left and right sides, valve leaflets/cords, thicker left-ventricular muscle, and complete directed pulmonary/systemic circuits. Great vessels are deliberately spread apart to make their origins visible.
- Additional heart views: open/closed valve states, coronary branches arising at the aortic root, right-atrial natural pacemaker, and an illustrative single-lead artificial pacemaker routed through a vein to the right heart.
- Vessels: transverse wall-layer comparisons and separate longitudinal views; valve flaps occur along the vein. Capillary walls are continuous single-cell layers with cell junctions, not a thick dashed wall with large holes. Tissue-fluid exchange and an interconnected capillary bed are separate views.
- SVG text describes the drawings for assistive technology. HTML keys remain readable on narrow screens. Each SVG has unique paint-server and title IDs, including its separate enlarged copy. Assessment diagrams omit the corresponding answer labels in both sizes.
