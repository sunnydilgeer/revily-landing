# Physics Lesson 55 storyboard — Investigating waves

Chapter P6, Waves. Folder `physics/lesson-55`, id `P-WAV-055-P`, skill `P-WAVEPRAC`, spec 6.6.1.2 (required practical: waves in a ripple tank and on a vibrating string). It owns the ripple tank method, how to find the wavelength and speed (ten wavelengths, divide by ten, v = fλ), the vibrating string method (loops are half wavelengths), why each set-up is suitable, and safety (water near electrics, falling masses, hot lamp). It prepares students for the practical and never claims to replace it.

Big idea: waves are hard to see, so each set-up makes the wave visible and lets you measure the wavelength without disturbing it. The frequency is whatever the signal generator is set to, so speed = frequency × wavelength.

Flow note: ripple tank set-up first, then the measuring and calculation, then the string as a second way, then safety in one place, so students meet each method whole before the safety points that cover both. Calculation flow: worked example in the frames (0.20 m across ten wavelengths, 10 Hz gives 0.20 m/s; string 0.80 m with 4 loops gives 0.40 m) → near-identical guided items (0.30 m and 5 Hz; 0.90 m with 3 loops) → independent items (0.24 m and 5 Hz; string 1.2 m with 4 loops).

Sections:
1. Start here (P55-01): waves you can see.
2. How does a ripple tank work? (P55-02–04): the tank → set the frequency → shadows → shadow lines. Checks: what sets the frequency; the lamp.
3. How do you find the ripple speed? (P55-05–07): ten wavelengths → divide by ten → v = fλ → why suitable. Checks: a guided speed calculation; why suitable.
4. How do you use a vibrating string? (P55-08–10): set-up → clear wave → count loops → find one wavelength. Checks: 3 loops on 0.90 m; why suitable.
5. How do you stay safe? (P55-11–12): water and electricity → falling masses → the lamp and your teacher. Check: string safety step.
6. On your own (P55-13–17): a string wavelength from a diagram (assessment view), a ripple speed, a spill, why ten wavelengths, and a written method.

Out of scope: uncertainty and error analysis; the vibrating string's standing-wave theory (loops are just half wavelengths); measuring frequency independently; the speed of sound method (previous lesson).

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 223 (scope only); AQA 8464 Physics 6.6.1.2 and the required practical on waves. All wording, numbers and diagrams are original. Draft pending teacher review.
