# Physics Lesson 1 storyboard — Energy stores and systems

Chapter P1, Energy. Folder `physics/lesson-1`, id `P-ENE-001-P`, skill `P-STORES`, spec 6.1.1.1. It owns the eight energy stores, the four ways energy is transferred, and systems and closed systems. Conservation of energy, dissipation and work done are taught in the next lesson.

Big idea: energy is never used up. It is kept in stores, and it moves between stores in four ways. Physicists describe any change by naming the store energy leaves, the store it reaches and how it moves.

Flow note: stores first (the nouns), then transfers (the verbs), then systems, because a student needs both words before they can describe what happens to a system. Store names and colours are the shared course set from PhysicsKit and stay identical in every Physics lesson.

Sections:
1. Start here (P1-01): food, a run for the bus, where does the energy go?
2. What are energy stores? (P1-02–05): the idea of a store, kinetic and thermal, chemical, gravitational and elastic potential, electrostatic and magnetic, nuclear (eight in all). Checks: kinetic, elastic potential, chemical.
3. How does energy move? (P1-06–08): a transfer between stores, heating and radiation, mechanically and electrically, describing a transfer. Checks: hot mug to hands, pushing a box.
4. What is a system? (P1-09–11): a system, a changing system, closed systems, the total stays the same. Checks: closed system, hot and cold blocks.
5. On your own (P1-12–15): a stores-only list, a numbered diagram question, a fan, then a written description of lifting a box.

Out of scope: conservation of energy and dissipation (next lesson); energy calculations (later lessons); Higher-tier ideas.

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 166 (scope only); AQA 8464 Physics 6.1.1.1. All wording, examples and diagrams are original (mug of tea, bath of hot water, hot and cold blocks, fan, box on a shelf). Draft pending teacher review.

Judgement calls for the teacher:
- Thermal store is used throughout; the phrase "internal energy store" is left for the particle-model lessons.
- "Radiation" is taught as light and sound, as the page says; it is not linked to infrared here.
- The toy-car example says energy moves electrically from the chemical store of the battery to the kinetic store of the car, which skips the motor; that is the level the page uses.
