# Physics Lesson 43 storyboard — Distance, displacement, speed and velocity

Chapter P5, Forces (motion). Folder `physics/lesson-43`, id `P-MOT-043-P`, skill `P-VELOCITY`, spec 6.5.4.1.1–6.5.4.1.3. It owns distance vs displacement, speed vs velocity, s = vt (substitution plus one rearrangement, v = s ÷ t), average speed and the typical speeds table.

Big idea: distance and speed are about size only; displacement and velocity also have a direction. Speed links distance and time: s = v × t.

Flow note: distance and displacement first (the "there and back" example is the key idea), then speed and velocity as the same split, then the equation once as a worked example with guided practice, then the rearranged form as a second short walkthrough, then typical speeds used for estimating. Calculation flow: s = vt worked (6 m/s, 20 s, 120 m) → guided (3 m/s, 50 s, 150 m) → independent (25 m/s, 40 s, 1000 m). v = s ÷ t worked (300 m, 12 s, 25 m/s) → guided (90 m, 60 s, 1.5 m/s) → independent (600 m, 20 s, 30 m/s).

Sections:
1. Start here (P43-01): a dog on a path, there and back.
2. Distance or displacement? (P43-02–04): distance, displacement, there and back, direction matters. Checks: Ali's walk; which is a vector.
3. Speed or velocity? (P43-05–06): speed, velocity, average speed. Check: two cars at 20 m/s in opposite directions.
4. How do you find distance? (P43-07–08): the equation, write, substitute, answer. Guided calculation.
5. How do you find speed? (P43-09–10): rearrange, substitute, answer. Guided calculation.
6. What are typical speeds? (P43-11–12): the six speeds, what changes speed, sound and wind. Estimating check.
7. On your own (P43-13–16): thrown ball (distance and displacement), independent speed, independent distance, written task.

Out of scope: rearranging for time; converting km/h or minutes; distance-time graphs (next lessons); vector addition of velocities; the equipment for measuring speed (a later practical).
