# Physics Lesson 42 storyboard — Investigating springs

Chapter P5, Forces. Folder `physics/lesson-42`, id `P-FOR-042-P`, skill `P-SPRINGPRAC`, spec 6.5.3 (required practical: force and extension of a spring). It owns the springs practical as preparation (aim, apparatus, variables, method, safety, results table, graph, limit of proportionality) and Ee = ½ke² as a recap with one worked example.

Big idea: add masses to a spring, measure the extension each time, and plot force against extension to see the straight line and where it bends. Then use Ee = ½ke² for the energy stored.

Flow note: aim, kit and variables first (so each step of the method has a reason), then the method in order, then safety just before the results (students know what can go wrong once they know the set-up), then the table and graph, then the energy recap which needs a spring and an extension. Calculation flow for Ee: worked (k 200, 5 cm, 0.25 J) → guided (k 80, 50 cm, 10 J) → independent (k 600, 10 cm, 3 J). The weight calculation (300 g → 2.94 N) and the extension subtraction are guided items in the method section.

Sections:
1. Start here (P42-01): masses hanging on a spring.
2. What are we testing? (P42-02–04): aim, apparatus, independent and dependent variable, control variables. Checks: the dependent variable; why the tape marker.
3. What is the method? (P42-05–07): no masses, find the force with W = mg, add and wait, extension, repeat. Checks: weight of 300 g; extension from two lengths.
4. How do you stay safe? (P42-08–09): goggles, falling masses, care. Check: the sensible safety step.
5. What do you do with the results? (P42-10–11): table, plot, at least five points, read the graph. Check: which axis is force.
6. How much energy is stored? (P42-12–13): the equation, convert and square, finish. Guided calculation.
7. On your own (P42-14–16): independent Ee calculation, results table read for the limit, written method.

Out of scope: line of best fit technique details; uncertainty; calculating the gradient; rearranging Ee; the real practical itself. This lesson prepares the practical and does not replace doing it.

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 208 (scope only); AQA 8464 Physics 6.5.3. All wording, numbers, examples and diagrams are original. Draft pending teacher review.

- Safety is proportionate: goggles, stable stand, soft tray, feet clear, do not overload. The real practical follows the school's risk assessment.
