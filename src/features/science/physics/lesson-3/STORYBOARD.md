# Physics Lesson 3 storyboard — Kinetic energy

Chapter P1, Energy. Folder `physics/lesson-3`, id `P-ENE-003-P`, skill `P-KINETIC`, spec 6.1.1.2. It owns the kinetic energy store idea (mass and speed) and the equation Ek = ½mv² by substitution.

Big idea: the energy in a kinetic store depends on mass and speed. It is worked out with Ek = ½ × m × v², squaring the speed first.

Flow note: the meaning first (mass, speed, speeding up and slowing down), then the equation as words, symbols and units, then a worked example, then two guided calculations with new numbers, then an independent calculation on your own. Calculation flow: worked example (0.5 kg at 4 m/s, 4 J) → near-identical guided items (2 kg at 3 m/s, 9 J; 40 kg at 5 m/s, 500 J) → independent (50 kg at 6 m/s, 900 J) → written (3 kg at 2 m/s, 6 J).

Sections:
1. Start here (P3-01): a lorry and a bike at the same speed.
2. What is kinetic energy? (P3-02–04): moving means kinetic energy, more mass, more speed, speeding up and slowing down. Checks: faster identical car; slowing cyclist.
3. What is the equation? (P3-05–07): the equation in words, symbols and units, square the speed first. Checks: what to do first; the units.
4. How do you work one out? (P3-08–10): write it down, square the speed, answer with joules; two guided calculations.
5. On your own (P3-11–14): two balls of different mass, an independent calculation, a numbered two-car diagram, a written calculation.

Out of scope: rearranging the equation to find speed or mass; calculating speed from height (too hard for Foundation); Higher-tier ideas. The exam question on the page was not reused.

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 168 (scope only); AQA 8464 Physics 6.1.1.2. All wording, numbers, examples and diagrams are original. Draft pending teacher review.

Judgement calls for the teacher:
- All numbers are given in kg and m/s, so no unit conversions arise.
- The idea that doubling the speed quadruples the energy is left out: the page does not ask for it.
- Answers with big numbers (for example 1800 J) appear only as distractors.

## Diagram specs
Look as Lessons 17 and 18: soft rounded shapes, `physicsPalette`, the kinetic store badge from PhysicsKit (same colour as everywhere). The equation is drawn as a card with the parts colour-coded, like the equation cards in the Chemistry lessons: kinetic energy (Ek), mass (m), speed (v). Text at least 12px.

- `kinetic-moving`: a rolling ball with motion lines and a "Kinetic" badge, beside a parked car with a "no movement, no kinetic energy" tag.
- `kinetic-mass`: a bike and a lorry side by side, both with the same speed arrow (equal length, label "same speed"); the lorry has a bigger kinetic badge / bar than the bike.
- `kinetic-speed`: two identical cars, one with a long speed arrow (labelled "faster") and a larger kinetic bar, the other with a short arrow and a smaller bar.
- `kinetic-change`: two panels. Left "speeds up": car with growing kinetic bar and an arrow into the badge. Right "slows down": car with shrinking bar and an arrow out of the badge, labelled "energy transferred away".
- `kinetic-words`: an equation card in words: "kinetic energy = ½ × mass × speed²" with each word in its own colour, and a small note "speed squared = speed × speed".
- `kinetic-symbols`: the same card in symbols "Ek = ½ × m × v²", with each symbol pointing to its name and unit: Ek (kinetic energy, joules, J), m (mass, kilograms, kg), v (speed, metres per second, m/s).
- `kinetic-square`: the card with "v²" highlighted and a small step strip: "1 square v", "2 × m", "3 × ½"; the numbers "3 m/s → 3 × 3 = 9" shown beside it. The mass is clearly not squared.
Worked example frames share one drawing: a friendly ball with a speed arrow labelled "4 m/s" and a tag "mass 0.5 kg", beside the equation card; a three-step strip along the top ("write it", "square", "answer"), the current step highlighted.
- `kinetic-work-1`: the ball, its data tags, the equation Ek = ½ × m × v² written under it.
- `kinetic-work-2`: the equation with numbers substituted, "Ek = ½ × 0.5 × 4²" then "4² = 4 × 4 = 16" highlighted, then "Ek = ½ × 0.5 × 16".
- `kinetic-work-3`: the calculation completed: "½ × 0.5 = 0.25", "0.25 × 16 = 4", answer "Ek = 4 J" in a highlighted box with the unit J circled.

- `kinetic-q-cars` (assessment view): two identical cars side by side labelled "Car 1" and "Car 2", both with the text "mass: 800 kg"; Car 1 has a short speed arrow labelled "8 m/s", Car 2 a long arrow labelled "20 m/s" (Car 2 is the correct answer). No kinetic bars or store badges. Neutral accessible description: "Two cars of the same mass with different speeds."
