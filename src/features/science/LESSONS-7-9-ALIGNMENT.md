## Assessment and product boundaries

- Choice questions are automatically marked from explicit authored answers.
- Extended explanations remain `teacherOnly` and can only become `pendingTeacherReview` in the prototype.
- A learner may continue while an explanation awaits review; the interface does not award secure explanation mastery.
- Every practical screen is preparation or interpretation. It does not claim completion of the hands-on required practical.
- Lessons 1–6 retain their A and B copies. Lessons 7–9 intentionally have no Variant A copy, and routing always preserves or adds `variant=b` for them.
