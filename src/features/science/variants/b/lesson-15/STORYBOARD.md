# Lesson 15 storyboard — Health and disease

Big idea: health is physical and mental well-being, and different health problems can affect each other.

1. Start here (B15-01): Sam's week — a cold, poor sleep and exam worry affect body and mind.
2. What health means (B15-02–03): Sam's week (invented, non-stigmatising); well-being (plain meaning first); health is more than having no disease.
3. Two kinds of disease (B15-04–06): communicable diseases are caused by pathogens and spread; non-communicable diseases cannot be caught (link to Lesson 14).
4. Diseases can affect each other (B15-07–09): a weak immune system makes infections more likely; some viruses can trigger some cancers (not every infected person); allergies such as skin rashes and asthma.
5. Body, mind and life (B15-10–12): long illness → depression (defined plainly); diet and stress; life situation (money, housing, access to healthcare).
6. On your own (B15-13–16): classify Disease X (no category words in the diagram); read a bar chart of example absence data; physical ill health affecting mental health; teacher-reviewed explanation.

Wording rules: one new term per frame, plain meaning first. Out of scope: fungi, protists and other pathogen detail (taught in B3); sampling principles.
