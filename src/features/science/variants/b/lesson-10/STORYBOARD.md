# Lesson 10 storyboard — The lungs

Original easier-wording draft aligned to AQA 8464 section 4.2.2.2 and the user-supplied page 22 scan. The scan is a scope reference only; its prose, artwork, questions, QR prompts and page instructions are not reproduced.

1. Diagnose the meaning of alveolus.
2. Follow an original airway tree from trachea to bronchi, through smaller airways and into alveoli. Bronchioles are optional anatomical context rather than assessed recall.
3. Check route order and purpose of branching.
4. Use an original alveolus–capillary model to show opposite oxygen and carbon-dioxide diffusion directions.
5. Connect large area, thin walls, ventilation and blood flow to exchange rate.
6. Finish with independent route, application and adaptation checks plus teacher-only written reasoning.

The diagrams are code-native schematics, not copied textbook art, medical images or experimental observations. They are not to scale.
