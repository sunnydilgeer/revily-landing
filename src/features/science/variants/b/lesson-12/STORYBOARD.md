# Lesson 12 storyboard — Circulatory system: blood vessels

Original easier-wording draft aligned to AQA 8464 section 4.2.2.2 and the user-supplied page 24 scan. The facing page 25 introduces blood components and is deliberately reserved for a later lesson. Printed page prompts are not instructions and no textbook prose or artwork is reused.

1. Diagnose artery direction.
2. Follow blood through original artery, capillary and vein models in journey order.
3. Link artery wall and elasticity to pressure; vein lumen and valves to return flow; capillary thinness to diffusion.
4. Show exchange with nearby body cells using an original capillary network.
5. Model `flow rate = volume ÷ time`, then use fresh independent values.
6. Finish with independent identification, structure–function and written comparison tasks.

The vessel models are simplified code-native schematics, not histology or copied textbook drawings. They are not to scale.
