# Lesson 9 storyboard — Digestion and food tests

Variant B only · 24 states · AQA 4.2.2.1 and required practical 3.

## Learning journey

The interface presents two explicit parts: **Part 1 — Digestion** and **Part 2 — Food tests**. This keeps the 24-state lesson in one numbered lesson while making the conceptual-to-practical transition visible.

1. Separate chemical digestion from absorption.
2. Match carbohydrases, proteases and lipases to substrates and products.
3. Locate where digestive enzymes are made and where they work.
4. Explain bile’s two roles: neutralising acid and emulsifying fat.
5. Prepare to test a food sample safely in a supervised laboratory.
6. Match Benedict’s, iodine, Biuret and ethanol-emulsion tests to their positive results.
7. Interpret combined observations and explain how digestion enables absorption.
8. Explain bile in a teacher-reviewed response.

## Visual plan

- Original digestion chains for starch, protein and lipid.
- A compact production/action-site table.
- A before/after emulsification comparison.
- Four code-native test cards; assessment views hide target and result labels.

## Method decision

The current AQA practical handbook’s ethanol-emulsion method is the primary lipid test. Sudan III is acknowledged as an alternative school method because it appears in the supplied scan, but learners are told to follow the method named by their teacher or question.

## Practical boundary

No home experiment is suggested. Heating, ethanol and reagents are placed behind school supervision, protective equipment and the centre’s risk assessment. The prototype teaches method knowledge and result interpretation only.
