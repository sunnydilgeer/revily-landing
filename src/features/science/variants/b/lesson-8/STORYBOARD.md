# Lesson 8 storyboard — Enzymes and reaction rates

Variant B only · 21 states · AQA 4.2.2.1 and required practical 4.

## Learning journey

1. Establish enzymes as reusable biological catalysts.
2. Use the simplified lock-and-key model to link active-site shape with specificity.
3. Explain low-temperature slowing, optimum conditions and high-temperature denaturation.
4. Link pH away from the optimum to active-site change.
5. Plan the amylase investigation: vary pH, control temperature and amounts, and sample into iodine every 30 seconds.
6. Calculate a scaled rate when a formula is supplied and interpret an original results set.
7. Explain the practical method in a teacher-reviewed response.

## Visual plan

- Original enzyme/substrate schematics and temperature/pH curves.
- A water-bath and spotting-tile diagram for continuous sampling.
- An original four-point dataset for time/rate interpretation.

## Practical boundary

The lesson prepares learners for a supervised school practical. It repeatedly states that the real investigation needs a risk assessment and teacher supervision. It does not record hands-on practical completion.
