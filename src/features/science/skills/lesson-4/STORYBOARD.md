# Working Scientifically Lesson 4 storyboard: Designing investigations

Strand skills, chapter WS1 "Working scientifically". Folder `skills/lesson-4`, id `W-MTH-004-W`, spec WS 2.1, 2.2. It owns hypothesis → prediction, the fair test, independent, dependent and control variables, the control experiment, and repeatable, reproducible and valid results.

Big idea: a fair test changes one thing, measures one thing and keeps everything else the same, so the evidence can be trusted.

Flow note: the idea chain first (observation, hypothesis, prediction, evidence), because variables only make sense once there is something to test. Then the fair test and the three kinds of variable, then the control experiment. Trust in results (repeatable, reproducible, valid) comes last because it needs a completed fair test to talk about. Examples come from all three sciences: ice on metal and wood, dissolving sugar, acid concentration, ramp and trolley, fertiliser and plants.

Sections:
1. Start here (W4-01): walking to school with a heavier bag; how to test it fairly.
2. From idea to prediction (W4-02–04): observation → hypothesis → prediction → testing it. Checks: pick the prediction; what mismatching evidence suggests.
3. How do you make it fair? (W4-05–08): fair test → independent → dependent → control variables → control experiment. Checks: independent variable; a control variable; purpose of a control.
4. Can you trust the results? (W4-09–11): repeatable → reproducible → valid. Checks: how to check repeatability; why unfair tests are not valid.
5. On your own (W4-12–15): dependent variable on a plant diagram; repeatable readings from a small table; spot the unfair test; written plan for a ramp and trolley.

Out of scope: choosing sample size and errors (next lesson); tables and averages; graphs; evaluating and uncertainty.

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide pages 236 and 237 (scope only; this lesson uses page 237); AQA 8464 WS 2.1, 2.2. All wording, examples, numbers and diagrams are original. Draft pending teacher review.

Judgement calls for the teacher:
- "Valid" is taught as fair test + repeatable + reproducible + answers the question, as on the page.
- "Repeat at least three times" is stated as a rule of thumb.

- `wsdesign-prediction`: three stacked boxes joined by arrows: observation, hypothesis, prediction (highlighted) with "ice on metal melts in less time than ice on wood". Short words only.

- `wsdesign-dependent`: the same ramp with a stopwatch highlighted at the bottom; label "dependent variable: the one you measure".
