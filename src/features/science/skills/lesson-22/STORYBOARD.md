# Working Scientifically Lesson 22 storyboard: Percentage change

Strand skills, chapter WS2 "Practical skills". Folder `skills/lesson-22`, id `W-PRC-022-W`, spec WS 4.6 and maths skills. It owns percentage change = (final − original) ÷ original × 100, what positive and negative mean, and comparing results with different starting values (potato cylinders, with an original example).

Big idea: percentage change turns results with different starting values into a fair comparison.

Flow note: the equation first, with a positive worked example; then negative changes, because the sign needs its own meaning; then comparing two results, which is the reason the skill exists. Calculation flow: worked example → near-identical guided item → independent calculation on your own, for increase, decrease and comparison. Links back: potato cylinders in osmosis (Biology) in one clause; means and significant figures (earlier lessons) not repeated.

Sections:
1. Start here (W22-01): the same gain from different starts.
2. How do you work out a percentage change? (W22-02–05): compare change fairly → the equation → which value is original. Worked: 8.0 g to 10.0 g = +25%. Guided: 50 g to 60 g = 20%. Check: what to divide by.
3. What does a negative change mean? (W22-06–09): positive = increase → negative = decrease. Worked: 5.0 g to 4.0 g = −20%. Guided: 20 g to 15 g = −25%. Check: meaning of −8%.
4. How do you compare two results? (W22-10–12): compare percentages → bigger gain is not always a bigger percentage. Worked: 6.0→7.2 g (+20%) against 10.0→11.5 g (+15%). Guided: 4.0→5.0 (+25%) against 20.0→24.0 (+20%).
5. On your own (W22-13–15): crystals 50 g to 40 g = −20%; a numbered-free table question comparing two cylinders (+20% against +15%); written task on two seedlings (20% against 15%).

Out of scope: percentage error, reverse percentages, compound change, graphs of percentage change.

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 255 (scope only); AQA 8464 WS 4.6. All wording, examples, numbers and diagrams are original (the page's potato table is not reused). Draft pending teacher review.

Judgement calls for the teacher:
- Every calculation is shown with the change, division and multiplication as separate lines, with no rounding needed.
- Answers are written with a plus sign in worked examples to reinforce the positive meaning; the guided answers use plain numbers.
