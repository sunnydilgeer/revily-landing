# Working Scientifically Lesson 5 storyboard: Collecting data

Strand skills, chapter WS1 "Working scientifically". Folder `skills/lesson-5`, id `W-DAT-005-W`, spec WS 2.3, 2.5, 2.6, 3.7. It owns sample size, precise vs accurate (definitions and a small table), resolution and right equipment, systematic vs random errors and anomalous results.

Big idea: good data comes from enough measurements, a good method and suitable equipment, and from knowing what kinds of error to look out for.

Flow note: how much data first, then how good each measurement is (precise vs accurate, method, equipment), then what goes wrong (errors and anomalies), which lets the student use the earlier words. Processing the data (mean, median) and sampling methods are left to later lessons. Examples are from Biology (plants, populations), Chemistry (gas volumes, cylinders) and Physics (balance, ruler, temperature).

Sections:
1. Start here (W5-01): 5 plants versus 50 plants.
2. How much data do you need? (W5-02–04): sample size → bigger is better → realistic and representative. Checks: why bigger; the sensible sample.
3. Precise or accurate? (W5-05–08): precise → accurate → three-student table → method → equipment. Checks: describe readings; gas syringe; the right cylinder.
4. What can go wrong? (W5-09–11): systematic → random → reduce with repeats and a mean → anomalous. Checks: spot the systematic error; reduce random errors.
5. On your own (W5-12–15): read a table of three students; an anomalous reading; a ruler error; written plan for trustworthy gas data.

Out of scope: random sampling methods (later lesson); calculating means and medians (Processing data); uncertainty and percentage error.

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 238 (scope only); AQA 8464 WS 2.3, 2.5, 2.6, 3.7. All wording, numbers, examples and diagrams are original; the page's table and cake joke are not reused. Draft pending teacher review.

Judgement calls for the teacher:
- Precise is defined as close together (close to each other and their own mean).
- "Throw away" of anomalies is framed as find the cause, then ignore in processing; readings are never changed.
