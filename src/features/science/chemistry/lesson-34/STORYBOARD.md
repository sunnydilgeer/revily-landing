# Chemistry Lesson 34 storyboard: The disappearing cross

Chapter C6, Rate and extent of chemical change. Folder `chemistry/lesson-34`, id `C-RAT-034-C`, skill `C-RATE-MEASURE-CROSS`. Required practical preparation (rate and concentration): it teaches the method, variables and data reading, and does not claim to complete the practical.

Big idea: some reactions make a solid (a precipitate) that clouds a clear solution. Timing how long a cross under the flask takes to disappear measures the rate: a shorter time means a faster reaction. The end point is a judgement, so the results are subjective. Higher concentration gives a shorter time.

Flow note: how and why the cross vanishes first, because the timing only makes sense once the precipitate is understood. Then the results and the concentration trend. Then fair, safe and reliable, because those points rely on the method. Gas collection and mass loss belong to the previous lesson; graphs and mean rate come later.

Sections:
1. Start here (C34-01): timing a mixture that goes cloudy.
2. The disappearing cross (C34-02–04): a solid appears → the cross → timing → subjective.
3. Concentration and time (C34-05–07): one change → table of results → higher concentration, less time.
4. Fair, safe and reliable (C34-08–10): same conditions → sulfur dioxide, ventilation → repeat and same judge.
5. On your own (C34-11–14): numbered apparatus, a data table, two timings differing, and a written method.

Out of scope: rate calculations and graphs (a later lesson); the ionic equation and the equation for sodium thiosulfate with acid; changing temperature or the thiosulfate concentration as a full second method (mentioned in one clause only).

Source boundary: supplied revision-guide page 139 (scope only). AQA 8464 Chemistry 5.6.1.2. All wording, examples and diagrams are original; the results table is the standard textbook style with original wording. Draft pending teacher review.

## Diagram specs
Drawing style: Chemistry palette (`atomPalette`), soft organic conical flask with rounded shoulders, gentle tints, hand-drawn-feeling curves. Colourless solutions are very pale blue-grey; sulfur precipitate is pale yellow. The black cross is `ink`. Reuse one flask drawing across frames.
- `cross-cloudy`: two clear flasks (labels "sodium thiosulfate solution", "hydrochloric acid", both "colourless") with an arrow to one flask with a pale yellow cloudy mixture and small yellow specks labelled "yellow solid (precipitate)".
- `cross-set`: conical flask with clear solution standing on paper with a black cross; a small stopwatch beside it; an eye/arrow labelled "look from above". Numbered steps 1 flask of sodium thiosulfate, 2 add acid and start stopwatch.
- `cross-time`: three flasks left to right at the start, part-way and end: clear with cross clearly visible, cloudier with cross faint, opaque yellow with cross gone; stopwatch stops on the last, label "stop the stopwatch".
- `cross-subjective`: two small speech-bubble people, one says "Gone now!", other says "I can still see it": a flask with a very faint cross between them. Title in text: "People may disagree".
- `cross-fair`: three flasks each on a cross, labelled with three acid concentrations (e.g. low, medium, high) and captioned "change: acid concentration"; a small "keep the same" box.
- `cross-table`: table "Concentration of hydrochloric acid (g/dm³)" 18, 36, 54, 72, 90 with "Time for cross to disappear (s)" 193, 184, 178, 171, 164.
- `cross-trend`: the same table with an arrow beside the concentration row pointing right labelled "higher concentration" and a matching arrow by the time row pointing to smaller numbers labelled "shorter time = faster reaction".
- `cross-same`: a checklist tile: same volume of thiosulfate, same volume of acid, same temperature, same flask, same cross, same lighting (small icons), with "only the acid concentration changes" highlighted.
- `cross-safe`: flask on a bench by an open window/fan; wispy grey curls labelled "sulfur dioxide gas" leaving the flask; safety goggles icon. Text "Well-ventilated place".
- `cross-repeat`: one person's face judging three flasks (three timings) with the same stopwatch; label "same person judges each time".
- Question visuals (assessment view, no words that answer): `cross-q-set` shows the flask on the cross seen from the side with numbered pointers 1 flask, 2 liquid, 3 black cross under the flask, 4 stopwatch; no labels. `cross-q-table` shows a table of three concentrations (for example 20, 50 and 80 g/dm³) with times 150 s, 95 s, 60 s and no comment about trend.
