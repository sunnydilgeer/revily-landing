# Lesson 7 storyboard — Cells, tissues and organs

Variant B only · 15 states · AQA 4.2.1 with digestive-system context from 4.2.2.1.

## Learning journey

1. Diagnose whether the learner recognises a tissue.
2. Build the five-level hierarchy from cell to organism.
3. Distinguish one epithelial cell from epithelial tissue.
4. Explain why the stomach is an organ.
5. Follow the digestive route and separate the roles of the liver, gall bladder, pancreas and intestines.
6. Apply the hierarchy to an unfamiliar structure and write a teacher-reviewed organ explanation.

## Visual plan

- A five-card hierarchy changes focus as each level is taught.
- Original epithelial-cell and simplified digestive-system schematics support explanation without copying the scans.
- The assessment hierarchy hides biological labels and shows neutral numbered levels.

## Evidence boundary

Independent evidence comes from classification, an organ-system explanation and a written bladder explanation. The final written response is saved for teacher review and is not auto-marked.
