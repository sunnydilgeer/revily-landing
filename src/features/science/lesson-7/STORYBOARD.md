# Lesson 7 storyboard — Cells, tissues and organs

Big idea: your body is built in levels, and each level is made from the one before: cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism.

Anchor / route: follow one sandwich. It goes down to the stomach, where we zoom in to the cells in the stomach wall, then build back up one level at a time: cells → tissues → the stomach as an organ → the organs the sandwich passes through → the digestive system → you, the organism. The ladder is taught once, going up; nothing is previewed and then re-taught.

Why this order:
- Cells come first because learners already know specialised cells from Lesson 4 (the muscle cell is the bridge).
- "Tissue" must exist before "organ", because an organ is defined as different tissues working together.
- The stomach is the first organ because we are already inside its wall; the rest of the route comes next, so "organ system" is defined only once learners have met several organs.
- "Explain a level" (exam technique) is the last frame before the final checks, so learners use it straight away.
