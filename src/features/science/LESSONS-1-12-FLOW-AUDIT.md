# Lessons 1–12 content-flow audit — 26 September 2026

Why lessons 13–26 (above all 17 and 18) flow better than lessons 1–12, and what to fix. Read-only audit; no lesson content changed. Screen IDs are state IDs in each `lesson-N/lesson.ts`. Nothing below removes safety notes, scientific caveats or the draft / teacher-review status.

## The shape lessons 13–26 follow (the checklist for every rewrite)

1. One big idea and one route, stated in the storyboard first (18: "follow the water from start to finish before switching to food").
2. One "Start here" question on an everyday or prior-lesson idea (B18-01 wet washing).
3. Ideas ordered by what each needs first, with the reason in the storyboard (guard cells B18-05 before "light opens stomata" B18-08).
4. Each section = one walkthrough of 3–6 frames, then 1–3 questions on exactly that content (B17-06 six layers → B17-07/08/09).
5. One new term per frame, plain meaning or process first, then "is called X".
6. Every frame: label, one-sentence summary, "Think: A → B" cue, 2–5 short sentences; frames chain with so/when/then; multi-part walkthroughs end with a "put it together" frame.
7. Sentences ≤ 26 words; other lessons referenced in one clause; no author notes in learner text.
8. Section titles are short plain questions or actions; the last is "On your own".
9. Wrong options are the misconceptions of the content just taught; hints are short questions pointing back to the frame; explanations are two steps, fact then "so".
10. Calculations: worked example → near-identical practice with new numbers → reuse in "On your own" (B18-11 → B18-12 → B18-17).
11. "On your own" = 3–4 independent items (new scenario, invented data read without over-claiming, numbered diagram or spot-the-error) + one written task with a method hint, rubric and reject lines.
12. Lessons hand on to each other explicitly ("You will learn more about them in the next lesson").

## Size

Lessons 13–26: 15–19 screens, 4–7 sections. Lessons 1–6: 24–45 screens (1: 42, 2: 34, 3: 30, 4: 24, 5: 31, 6: 45). Lessons 7–12: 14–24 screens.

## Lesson findings

### 1 Cells — split into 2
- B1-10/B1-11 "Models and observations" previews drawing from a microscope before microscopes exist; lesson 3 re-teaches it (B3-14 f3, B3-16 f2, B3-27). B1-09 belongs with it.
- Plant cells re-teach the animal cell: B1-24 spends 3 of 4 frames on membrane/cytoplasm/nucleus; B1-42 opens with two more; B1-41 "Compare" (no question) repeats the "five shared parts" list a third time.
- Recall B1-12…B1-18 (7 in a row) comes ~20 screens after B1-02/B1-05; B1-32…B1-40 (9 in a row) comes after the exam-style block B1-19/B1-20.
- B1-29 packs mm↔µm, ratio, order of magnitude and standard form into 4 frames; B1-30/B1-31 add area. B2-15 and B2-23 re-teach it.
- Written task B1-21 (animal vs bacterial) is never taught; the only comparison section B1-41 is animal vs plant.
- Plan: (a) animal and plant cells, (b) bacteria, eukaryotic/prokaryotic and a combined comparison. Units, standard form and area move to microscopy; B1-10/B1-11 move to lesson 3.

### 2 Microscopy — split into 2
- B2-02 f3 and B2-03 use magnification before B2-04 defines it; B2-12 f2 says "first match the units" a section before "Match the units" (B2-15).
- Worked examples repeat the frames with the same numbers: B2-12/B2-13 (12 ÷ 0.03), B2-15/B2-16, B2-18/B2-19 (15 ÷ 500), B2-20/B2-21 (0.04 × 250).
- Electron microscope ideas repeated: B2-08 f2–3 vs B2-09 f2 and B2-11; B2-08 has no question.
- B2-12…B2-24 is five formula sections; "Find real size" (B2-18/B2-19) has no question. 9 questions in a row at the end (B2-25…B2-33).
- Plan: (a) light vs electron microscopes (B2-01…B2-11), (b) magnification maths (B2-12…B2-24 plus lesson 1's units and standard form), each worked example followed by new-number practice.

### 3 Practical skills — restructure
- B3-12 → B3-14 (T3) → B3-15 (T2) → B3-16 (T4): 12 frames, one question (B3-13). "Observe onion cells" and "Observe animal cells" have no check.
- B3-15 switches to a prepared animal slide with no set-up; written task B3-30 covers only onion preparation.
- B3-16 f4 introduces scale before "Measure and add a scale" (B3-19); B3-08 f2 and B3-21 re-teach total magnification.
- "Draw only what you see" appears in B3-14 f3, B3-16 f2 and B3-27 (and lesson 1). Keep one strong instance; keep all safety and "does not replace doing it" lines.
- 8 questions in a row (B3-22…B3-29).
- Plan: one route prepare → look → draw → scale with a check after each; written task covers the whole method.

### 4 Specialisation — restructure

- Five 2-frame walkthroughs alternate with single questions.
- B4-16 f3, B4-21, B4-23 cover division, stem cells and meristems before lesson 5, which re-teaches them (B5-12 f2, B5-16 f2).
- Differentiation gets one T3 and 2 questions at the very end.
- Plan: open with "shape fits job"; one 3–4 frame walkthrough per cell then 1–2 questions; hand stem cells to lesson 5.

### 5 Cell division — split into 2
- "Chapter N ·" sections; the stem-cell section is 13 screens.
- Cell cycle split into B5-05, B5-07, B5-09 (T2 each) + W3 B5-11: one story in four scraps.

- Plan: (a) chromosomes and mitosis (B5-01…B5-13), (b) stem cells (B5-14…B5-30) with an evidence frame before B5-23.

### 6 Transport and exchange — split into 3
- Three transport processes, SA:V, five exchange surfaces, required practical 2, percentage change, graphs, rate, anomalies and mean.
- RP2 (B6-25) tests osmosis (B6-11) with active transport and exchange surfaces (B6-13…B6-24) in between.
- B6-28 worked example shows the percentage-change method; B6-30 f1 then repeats it. "Practical data · calculations" (B6-28…B6-45) is a 9-screen maths block.
- Dense frames: B6-04 f1, B6-06 f2, B6-30 f2 (axes, scales, plotting and trend line together), B6-21 f1; heavy terms up front in B6-02 f1 and B6-08 f1.
- B6-45 (anomalous +24% repeat) has no teaching; B6-19/B6-20 put a cube calculation into the exchange-surface story. 9 questions + written at the end.
- Plan: (a) diffusion, osmosis and RP2 with its data (B6-01…B6-12, B6-25…B6-45), (b) active transport and comparing the three processes (B6-13…B6-17), (c) SA:V and exchange surfaces (B6-18…B6-24).

## Common problems in 1–12
1. Tested before taught, or never taught (lessons 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12).
2. Taught twice, within lessons and across them (units 1↔2, alveoli 6↔10, bile 7↔9, stem cells 4↔5, coronary arteries 11↔14).
3. Runs of 7–9 questions saved for the end.
4. Topic-label titles, "Chapter N ·" labels, no anchor; the 9→12 transport story is not told.
5. Frames with 2–3 new terms or several ideas; author notes in learner text (7, 10, 11).
6. Maths and practicals as separate blocks after the biology.
