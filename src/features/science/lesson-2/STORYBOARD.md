# Lesson 2 — Microscopy: seeing cells and measuring them

Approved outline: introduce a light microscope; distinguish magnification from resolution; introduce an electron microscope separately before comparison; scaffold magnification, unit conversions, missing sizes and standard form; finish with independent questions and written reasoning.

AQA 8464 Foundation boundary: 4.1.1.5, WS 1.1, MS 1a/1b/2h/3b and WS 4.4. Official Biology subject content rechecked 14 September 2026: https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/biology-subject-content. The supplied CGP screenshot is a scope reference, not content to reproduce. Language, questions and illustrations are original Revily drafts.

## Sequence and visual plan

- B2-01–03: accessible opening check, dedicated light-microscope illustration and lens-magnification check. Introduce the whole instrument before its parts.
- B2-04–07: illustrate the same cell at two magnifications; separately illustrate two close features merged/separated to explain resolution. Short guided checks after each concept.
- B2-08–11: dedicated electron-microscope schematic, then comparison and development/understanding checks. No fake micrographs or claims that all structures are visible with a school microscope.
- B2-12–14: distinguish image size/real size; introduce magnification = image size ÷ real size. Replayable worked example with matching units, then a similar question.
- B2-15–17: retrieve mm/µm conversion, replay a mixed-unit example, then practise.
- B2-18–22: separately teach finding real size and image size, each with a worked example. All supplied image dimensions are explicit numbers, not measurements of a responsive screen.
- B2-23–24: small decimals and standard form, building on Lesson 1.
- B2-25–34: independent recall/understanding/application/calculation questions and a two-mark written explanation saved pending review.
