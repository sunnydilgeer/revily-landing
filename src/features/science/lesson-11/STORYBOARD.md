# Lesson 11 storyboard — Circulatory system: the heart

Original easier-wording draft aligned to AQA 8464 section 4.2.2.2 and the user-supplied page 23 scan. Printed page instructions are not product requirements, and no textbook prose or artwork is reused.

1. Bridge from the lung lesson by asking where oxygenated blood travels next.
2. Trace one continuous journey through the four chambers and the aorta, vena cava, pulmonary artery and pulmonary vein.
3. Compare ventricular walls and connect thickness to pressure and distance.
4. Recognise the completed journey as two linked circuits: heart–lungs–heart and heart–body–heart.
5. Explain one-way valves and the coronary supply to the heart muscle.
6. Separate the natural right-atrial pacemaker from an artificial electrical device.
7. Finish with independent route, vessel, valve and written whole-journey tasks.

All heart and circulation graphics are original code-native teaching schematics. They are simplified, not anatomical plates and not to scale.
