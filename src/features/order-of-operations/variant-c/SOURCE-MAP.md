## Mathematical and terminology decisions

- Division/multiplication and addition/subtraction are equal-priority pairs evaluated left to right. The existing Variant B examples remain necessary because the tutor sources do not fully teach both pairs.
- A fraction bar groups the complete numerator and denominator and then denotes division. Working top first is a useful workflow, not a mathematical requirement.
- N2.2 Q5c is only true when the single-line rewrite keeps explicit brackets around the numerator and denominator.
- The video phrase “multiply the letters” is not copied. Variant C says “multiply the coefficients and combine the variable factors.”
- In algebra, an expression such as `x²` cannot be numerically evaluated without a value of `x`; it is carried as a factor while products are simplified.
- “Multiply each term” is presented as “form each product,” avoiding the ambiguity between terms and factors.
