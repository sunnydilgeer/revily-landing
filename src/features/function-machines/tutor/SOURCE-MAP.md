# Lesson 29 (Algebra A14) source map

A14 is written from Aniksha's three PDF/video pairs `A14.1_Function_Machines_Input_To_Output`, `A14.2_Function_Machines_Output_To_Input` and `A14.3_Creating_Function_Machines` (zip `A14_v1`). It is lesson 29 in the course (`?lesson=29`, progress key `L029`; lesson 26 is kept for GR1), and students see it as lesson 14 of the Algebra chapter.

| Rung | Source | App coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input to output | A14.1 | Video + worked example = Q1 (7 → × 4 → + 3 → 31 sweets); Q2 (£24), Q3 (£7), Q4a (£22), Q4b (−18), Q5a (11), Q5b (−7) typed; Q5c (Aisha) typed as the two outputs, ① 19 and ② 33 |
| Output to input | A14.2 | Video + worked example = Q1 (? → × 5 → − 4 → 26 gives 6 miles); Q2 (£20), Q3 (£8), Q4a (24), Q4b (−28), Q5a (8) typed; Q5b (show 8 is right) typed as the output, 9; Q5c (Ben) as a choice |
| From an equation | A14.3 | Video + worked example = Q1 (y = 3x + 4 is × 3, then + 4); Q2, Q3, Q4a and Q5a (fill in the machine) as choices; Q4b (y = 4) and Q5b (y = 7) typed; Q5c (Dev) typed as the two values, y = 10 and Dev’s 18 |

Each working draws the question's machine above the A5 board (`MachinePictures.tsx`): the word Input, one box per operation (box 1 amber, box 2 blue, box 3 teal, as in the videos) and the word Output, joined by arrows, with the numbers underneath. The box being worked on and the number it makes are ringed in purple at the same moment as the board writes that box's sum, with the operation in purple. Going backwards, each box's opposite is written under it. Practice questions show their own machine above the answer box.

Every source answer was checked; all three videos' sums are right. The videos are Aniksha's files unchanged.
